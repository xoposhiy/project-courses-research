## Ideas

1. `1-agency`. The main danger of AI is the loss of student agency. The student gradually stops being the author of the solution and becomes an observer of what the agent does. The result can look good even when the student has no understanding and cannot continue the work alone. LLMs amplify the illusion of competence, so students do not notice right away that they are losing agency.

2. `2-context`. The key AI skill is context engineering. AI pushes toward more structured work: specifications, documents, and so on.

3. `3-strengths`. LLM strengths that students must learn to use:

   1. `3.1-expand`: they expand the space of ideas;

   2. `3.2-domain`: they use domain context;

   3. `3.3-critic`: they find possible problems and contradictions;

   4. `3.4-rl`: everything that is or will be covered by reinforcement learning: programming, math, tool use, games.

4. `4-weaknesses`. LLM weaknesses that students must learn to control and compensate for:

   1. `4.1-fuzzy`: tasks with a fuzzy boundary of correctness

   2. `4.2-priorities`: setting priorities: what matters and what does not.

   3. `4.3-horizon`: tasks with a long horizon of effect.

   4. `4.4-architecture`: application architecture.

   5. `4.5-project-idea`: choosing an idea for the project.

5. `5-discovery`. Nobody fully knows yet how to use all of this well, so we must teach students to experiment and form their own opinion.
