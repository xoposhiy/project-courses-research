A lifelong dream of mine realized! I'm teaching a new computer science course at Stanford this fall: CS 146S The Modern Software Developer.

Nearly 15 years after I fell in love with CS during undergrad, I get to go back to my alma mater to teach the next generation of computer scientists.

What's the course about?

In the last few years, large language models have introduced a revolutionary new paradigm in software development. From new-age IDEs and terminals to code review and testing platforms, every phase of the software life cycle is getting revamped. In this class, students will master both the theory behind traditional software engineering challenges and how to effectively use cutting-edge AI-powered tools to solve them. Students will learn the skills and perspective to thrive in the era of modern AI-driven development.

The course will also feature a rockstar lineup of industry pioneers building the future of software development including:

- @zachlloydtweets, CEO @warpdotdev
- Boris Cherny, Creator of @AnthropicAI Claude Code
- @0xine, CEO @semgrep
- @TomasReimers, CPO @withgraphite
- @EricSimons, CEO @boltdotnew
- Mayank Agarwal, CTO @resolveai
- @martin_casado , Partner @a16z

This course has been 10 months in the making and it is the first of its kind dedicated to AI software principles and practice.

Thank you to everyone that helped with its design and provided invaluable feedback: Jerry Cain (my undergraduate advisor who inspired my love for CS), @chipro , @arunchaganty, @samspelsberg, and others.
