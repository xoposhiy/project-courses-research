Students are not required to purchase a textbook for this class; all
required readings will be available for free in digital form. But there
is much to learn about software engineering beyond what we can cover in
lecture, and much of that knowledge is contained in books. Here are a
few recommended references:

Books

- Software Engineering at Google. Titus Winters, Tom Manshreck, Hyrum
  Wright. 2020.
- Better Embedded System Software. Philip Koopman. 2010.
- The Mythical Man-Month. Frederick P. Brooks, Jr. 1975.
- Software Engineering, Tenth Edition. Ian Sommerville. 2015.
- The Pragmatic Programmer, 20th Anniversary Edition. David Thomas,
  Andrew Hunt. 2019.

Online resources

- Mozilla Developer Network Web Docs
